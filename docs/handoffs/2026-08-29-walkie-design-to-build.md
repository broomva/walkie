## Build order

Milestone 1 in the architecture doc is *host agent, one workspace, no voice* and does not
need the client at all. The client can start in parallel, in this order:

1. **Tokens and the component set.** `c/StatusBar`, `c/AddressBar`, `c/Dock`, plus
   `ChannelLine`, `TurnLine`, `ThreadTurn`, `OptionLine`, `LifecycleRail`. Every screen is
   these seven plus text. Get them right and the screens assemble.
2. **The orb.** Port `orb.glsl` to MSL behind a SwiftUI `Shader`. It is one file with
   thirteen uniforms; do not build a second orb for any state.
3. **Home, Voice, Thread.** The spine. `HOME ⇄ VOICE ⇄ THREAD` covers the ordinary day.
4. **Pair, Microphone, Connecting, Off tailnet, Host offline.** Nothing works until the
   tailnet and the host are reachable, so this is where real integration starts.
5. **The ask loop.** Ask low-stakes → answered → undo. Then Look → approved / sent back.
6. **Ambient layer.** Live Activity, Dynamic Island, widgets. Last, because it needs the
   reduced signal and a working session to report on.

## The orb, concretely

One shader, three size presets, two themes. The uniforms are the runtime, not styling:

- `u_in` ← `getInputVolume()` — lifts the **whole** lattice. Undirected, because your
  speech arrives from outside the sphere.
- `u_out` ← `getOutputVolume()` — lifts **only the band it is travelling through**.
  Directed, because its speech is being produced somewhere.
- `u_work` / `u_paused` — the Undertow and the Understop. Identical motion, different
  palette. These never react to sound.
- `u_bleed` — weather inside the sphere as well as around it. On above 88pt, off below.
- `u_body` / `u_shade` / `u_sphere` / `u_ink` / `u_agent` — theme-driven; see tokens.

**Size presets** (`WalkieOrbSize`): dock `radius 0.66`, no weather, body forced in both
themes — a bodiless lattice at 44pt is dust. Inline `0.46`. Hero `0.26`, where the sphere
stays ~50pt and the *weather* is what scales.

**Porting notes.** The math is mechanical; `gl_FragCoord`, `texture2D` and the `@sdf`
uniform are the only things that need translating. Two bugs are already fixed in the
source and must not be reintroduced: output is **premultiplied** (`vec4(rgb, a)` after an
over-composite chain, never `rgb * a` a second time), and dot radius is a **fraction of the
sphere**, never absolute pixels. A 520-point loop hung the canvas renderer; 260 is the
tested figure and Metal will take more if you measure it.

## Grounded against Genesis

The design was drafted against the architecture document; it has since been checked against
`apps/genesis` itself. Three corrections landed, and they matter for anyone writing client code:

- **The phase machine is Genesis'.** `RunPhase = "idle" | "running" | "awaiting" | "blocked" | "done"`
  in `packages/projection/src/reducer.ts`. Running/Needs you/Stuck/Done map to
  running/**awaiting**/**blocked**/done. Queued and Standing have no Genesis phase — do not
  invent one.
- **`awaiting` is entered by `AskUserQuestion`,** and the reducer captures `pendingQuestion`
  off that tool call. A walkie ask *is* that tool invocation and its options are the tool's
  options. Build the ask screens against the tool schema, not against a parsed message.
- **The transport is a WebSocket** at `/ws?thread=<id>`, not SSE. Branches are
  `genesis/<key>`, not `run/<key>`. `noWorktree` is a sticky per-session posture, not a
  constant. `confined` is the real spawn-hardening flag and is now on the workspace screen.

**What Genesis does not have,** and is therefore ours to build rather than call: the gate and
the approve verb, the voice-reachability allowlist, standing routines, the append-only
per-user ask log, containment proof, and the orchestrator plane. `POST /control` gives
`reset · interrupt · status · archive · unarchive · rename` — Interrupt maps, Send back is a
`POST /message`, Approve has no counterpart. Any screen showing one of those is a
specification, not an integration.

The canvas board **`Wiring · what Genesis actually provides`** carries the full endpoint list
and the type signatures.

## Do not re-decide

These are load-bearing and were argued to a conclusion. Changing one is a product decision,
not an implementation detail.

1. **Nothing consequential is authorisable by voice.** Force-push, merge, dropping a
   migration, deleting. A completed run may be approved by voice *only* where the host
   proves containment — no merge, no push, no delete, revertible in one command — and that
   proof is computed from the diff and target by the service. Never asserted by the model,
   never inferred from a summary produced by the agent that wants the yes.
2. **An ask is structured data.** Ticket id, an enum, bounded fields. The service must be
   *structurally unable* to originate a workspace call from a worker-supplied string. This
   is why the dispatch card shows four typed lines and not a sentence.
3. **The orchestrator is never on the critical path.** *What is blocked*, *read me the last
   turn* and every ask answer come from Postgres in under two seconds regardless of what
   the orchestrator is doing. If it ever answers those, serialization and compaction kill
   the design exactly as the original document said they would.
4. **Colour belongs to work, size and brightness to voice.** Running is blue, paused is
   cyan, and the two never share a hue. A halted card wearing the running signal is a lie.
5. **Never draw progress.** No percentages, no fill levels, no bars. The lifecycle rail is
   five named stages. A half-full sphere was tried and rejected for exactly this reason.
6. **The thread is the audit artifact.** Every spoken exchange writes to it. That is what
   makes voice acceptable for thinking rather than only for triage — and it is a projection
   of the store, never the record.

## Open — decide before the screen ships

- **Reduced motion.** Not written. The Undertow needs a frozen mid-breath branch; the
  Understop is nearly there already. A shader does not stop for free the way a keyframe does.
- **Type scale.** The canvas runs 11/13/15/17; the design system specifies 12/14/16/18/22/24/28.
  Flagged three times and deliberately not swept, because it moves every screen.
- **Pair-failed** has no screen. Notification permission and mic-denied now do.
- **Thread and receipts empty states** have no screens.
- **No account concept anywhere.** Possibly correct — the tailnet is the identity — but it
  is undecided rather than decided.

## Known limitations of this handoff

- **Exports are dark theme only.** The canvas renders one theme at a time and there is no
  export-per-theme API. Light versions must be re-exported after switching the mode axis.
- **Two boards do not render** in the authoring environment — `iOS · Dynamic Island` and
  `iOS · Widgets`. Their data is correct and every child reports sane bounds; the renderer
  applies a spurious +50px offset to their children. Treat the *ambient layer* board and the
  lock-screen composition as the reliable reference for those three surfaces.
- **The ambient layer is designed, not specified.** Live Activity update budgets were not
  verified against current iOS limits, and the PushToTalk framework's entitlement and
  push-type specifics were not checked this session. Verify both before planning around them.
